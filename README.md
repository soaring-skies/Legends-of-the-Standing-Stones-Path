# Legends-of-the-Standing-Stones
Legend of the Standing Stones: Hollow Roads Path
Enter the Hollow Roads — ancient paths ringed by standing stones, where the dead still walk.
You move through a closed stone space of walls and corridors. Ghosts drift the paths and close in. Fight them off, grab power-ups before they fade, and survive as the roads grow more dangerous.
Features
Arena of walls and stone paths inspired by standing-stone legend
Ghost enemies that hunt you through the space
Power-ups to grab mid-run for a fighting chance
Simple, fast controls — play in the browser
Works on phone and desktop
How to play
Move through the stone space, avoid or defeat the ghosts, and collect power-ups to stay alive on the Hollow Roads.
No install — open and play.
